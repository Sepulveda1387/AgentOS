# Executive Board Framework

Use this reference when running a multi-executive review. Adapt each lens to the user's domain and priorities as defined in `context/about-user.md`, `context/about-system.md`, and `context/priorities.md`.

---

## CEO Lens

Optimize for direction, focus, strategic fit, sequencing, partnerships, and opportunity cost.

Questions to ask:
- Does this move toward the user's 90-day priorities?
- Does it create focus or create distraction?
- What should be done now, later, delegated, or declined?
- What is the biggest strategic tradeoff?

Preferred CEO output:
- Clear yes/no/modify stance.
- One strategic reason.
- One sequencing recommendation.
- One explicit "do this next" directive.

---

## CFO Lens

Optimize for revenue quality, pricing, cash timing, profitability, financial risk, and measurable targets.

Questions to ask:
- Does this create or protect revenue or value?
- Is the price, effort, and return reasonable?
- What payment, budget, or scope boundary is needed?
- What metric should be tracked?
- What financial risk or hidden cost could appear?

Preferred CFO output:
- Revenue or value implication.
- Cost and capacity implication.
- Measurement or payment recommendation.
- Scope, pricing, or margin guardrail when relevant.

---

## CMO Lens

Optimize for audience clarity, message, trust, distribution, conversion, and follow-up.

Questions to ask:
- Will the target audience understand the value quickly?
- Does the message fit the user's context and audience?
- Does it build trust without sounding abstract or overpromising?
- What channel, asset, or follow-up path should support it?
- What buying trigger or adoption signal does this connect to?

Preferred CMO output:
- Messaging implication.
- Audience fit assessment.
- Conversion or distribution next action.
- Specific copy or funnel recommendation when relevant.

---

## COO Lens

Optimize for repeatability, delivery reliability, owner capacity, handoffs, SOPs, and operational risk.

Questions to ask:
- Can this be delivered reliably with current capacity?
- What breaks if this scales or repeats?
- What handoff, checklist, or SOP is missing?
- What operational risk is being ignored?

Preferred COO output:
- Delivery confidence rating.
- Capacity or dependency gap.
- One SOP or process recommendation.
- One operational risk with mitigation.

---

## CPO Lens

Optimize for outcome quality, service or product design, packaging, usability, feedback loops, and roadmap clarity.

Questions to ask:
- Does the user or client get a clear outcome?
- Is the service or product well-packaged and easy to understand?
- What feedback loop is missing?
- What should be on the roadmap next?

Preferred CPO output:
- Outcome clarity assessment.
- Packaging or delivery recommendation.
- One feedback loop to add.
- One roadmap suggestion.

---

## Optional Lenses

Add these only when the prompt clearly requires them:

| Lens | Use when |
|------|----------|
| **CTO** | Integrations, automation, security, build-vs-buy, technical debt |
| **CRO** | Sales pipeline, deal strategy, revenue expansion, follow-up |
| **Legal/Risk** | Compliance, contracts, exposure, liability |
| **CDO/Data** | KPIs, dashboards, attribution, data quality, reporting |
| **Customer Success** | Onboarding, retention, churn risk, client health |
| **Partnerships/BD** | Alliances, channels, co-marketing, network expansion |

---

## Board Tension Checklist

Surface at least one real tension per review. Common patterns:

- Growth vs. current capacity
- Speed vs. quality
- Revenue now vs. margin later
- Marketing promise vs. delivery readiness
- Automation vs. human judgment
- Scope expansion vs. focus
- Short-term revenue vs. long-term positioning
